Fixed sponsored charges failing pre-broadcast simulation with "insufficient fee token balance" on Tempo nodes v1.18 and later, which bill the fee payer recovered from `feePayerSignature` in `eth_call` and the sender when it is absent. The final sponsored simulation now sends the fee payer's signature, as mppx does: a hosted fee payer's as returned, and a local fee payer's over a simulation-only copy with up to 6,000 more gas, clamped to the fee payer policy's gas and total-fee caps and signed only after the sender's transaction passes simulation. The broadcast transaction keeps its gas limit, and the sender simulation now runs at that exact limit with no fee fields, so an under-gassed transaction is rejected before any sponsor signs. A sponsored simulation without the full 6,000 gas of headroom (hosted, or clamped) tolerates an out-of-gas-only failure, since the node's sponsored call skips fee collection and costs 6,000 more gas than the mined transaction when its calls touch the fee token; fee-balance errors and reverts still reject. As mppx does, that failure is recognized only from the node's own JSON-RPC error message starting with "out of gas" or "intrinsic gas too low", never from a revert (code 3), an error with revert data or a wrapper's text.
