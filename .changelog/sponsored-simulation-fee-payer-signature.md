Fixed sponsored charges failing pre-broadcast simulation with "insufficient fee token balance" on Tempo nodes v1.18 and later, which bill the fee payer recovered from `feePayerSignature` in `eth_call` and the sender when it is absent. The final sponsored simulation now sends the fee payer's signature, as mppx does: a hosted fee payer's as returned, and a local fee payer's over a simulation-only copy with up to 10,000 more gas, clamped to the fee payer policy's gas and total-fee caps and signed only after the sender's transaction passes simulation. The broadcast transaction keeps its gas limit, and the sender simulation now runs at that exact limit with no fee fields, so an under-gassed transaction is rejected before any sponsor signs. A sponsored simulation without the full 10,000 gas of headroom (hosted, or clamped) tolerates an out-of-gas-only failure, since the node's sponsored call costs about 6,000 more gas than the mined transaction; fee-balance errors and reverts still reject.
