Changed `Fetch`, `PaymentMiddleware` and `ChargeClient` to pay a 402 without a request-level `Accept-Payment` header by default (`AcceptPaymentPolicy::Always`), as mppx does; `AcceptPaymentPolicy::Explicit` stays selectable, including through the new `ChargeClient::with_accept_payment_policy`. Added an `on_challenge` approval hook (`ClientEvents::set_on_challenge`, `PaymentMiddleware::with_on_challenge`, `ChargeClient::with_on_challenge`) that approves, declines (returning the 402 unpaid) or supplies a credential before each payment, as mppx's `onChallenge` does; while a hook is set, session providers send no prepaid vouchers before a 402 and start no channel top-up ahead of the next request (the client records receipts with the new `PaymentProvider::record_receipt_without_prepay`), and the hook receives the URL the credential is sent to. A 402 answering a credential with `invalid-challenge` (the replay answer), an empty 402 (how the default Tower layer answers a rejected credential), or a problem whose type cannot be read, was returned without paying again, also when the credential was one the caller set on the request (`Authorization: Payment`, including after another scheme in the same value, or `Payment-Authorization`), as mppx does, and an `invalid-challenge` 402 was not paid even when the credential came from the client's default headers; the client read at most 64 KiB of that problem body, and sends no prepaid credential over a caller's own. `McpClient` likewise returns a paid call's answer without paying again, and does not pay an `invalid-challenge` error that answers the caller's own credential when the new `ToolCaller::payment_problem_from_error` reports it.
