Fund machine-token charges with MACH through its canonical swapper, matching mppx; the legacy machineUSD session route keeps its deployment. `TempoAccountsProvider::with_preferred_currency` now selects the first charge or session funded from the preferred currency across intents, including machine-token-enabled charges when the preferred currency is MACH. Add `with_fee_token` and `with_session_fee_token` so sessions denominated in a recipient-restricted token, such as MACH channels under TIP-1095, can pay unsponsored gas in another token. Add `TempoSessionProvider::acknowledge_accepted` so metered SSE responses, whose `payment-receipt` and `payment-need-voucher` events report acceptance in the body rather than a `Payment-Receipt` header, can resolve a pending channel open, and `TempoAccountsProvider::session` to reach the session provider that pays its challenges. A pending open whose authorization is already settled on-chain is resolved before the next voucher and on restore. `TempoAccountsProvider` reuses one settlement RPC client per URL instead of opening a connection for every charge, and machine-token route discovery reads the balance and simulates the route concurrently.
