mpp: minor
---

Changed servers to follow the core spec: a replayed Credential got 402 with a fresh Challenge and `invalid-challenge`, Challenges carried a random `_mpp_nonce` in `opaque` (`Mpp::with_nonce(false)` turns it off; `_mpp_nonce` and `_mppx_nonce` are ignored when matching `opaque`, and a route `opaque` that is not a JSON object, or whose numbers JCS would rewrite, is issued without one; servers from earlier releases reject Challenges carrying `_mpp_nonce`, so during a rolling upgrade an older replica rejects Challenges a newer one issued), secret keys shorter than 32 bytes were refused, method names other than lowercase a-z were rejected, more than one Payment credential, or more than 32 `Authorization` and `Payment-Authorization` values or 128 KiB of them, got 400 `bad-request` without a Challenge, a credential in a field the Challenge didn't select was ignored, binding was checked before expiry, the default realm became `localhost` instead of `"MPP Payment"` (as pympp does), and problem titles used the spec's text such as "Payment Required". Added `Mpp::try_new`, `select_payment_credential` and the narrower `CredentialError` and `ConfigError` types.
