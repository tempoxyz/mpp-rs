Added Zone payments to the Tempo charge method, as mppx does. A server accepts charges paid from Zones with `TempoBuilder::zones` (`Zones::ids([3, 7])` or `Zones::Any`), which advertises them in `methodDetails.zones` and binds them to the route. A `zone` Credential must come from a Zone its challenge's `methodDetails.zones` accepts, so a route that issues challenges for other Zones with `charge_challenge_with_options` keeps verifying them, and unbound APIs such as `broadcast_credential` also reject a withdrawal from a Zone outside the handler's Zone ID list. It verifies the new `zone` Credential (`PayloadType::Zone`, `PaymentPayload::zone`): the Zone portal's `WithdrawalProcessed` event for the sender tag in the settlement transaction, and the `processWithdrawals` calldata paying the amount and currency to the recipient with a challenge-bound memo, once per withdrawal. A client pays from a Zone balance with `TempoProvider::with_zone(TempoZone::new(zone_rpc_url, zone_chain_id)?)`: it withdraws to the recipient with a challenge-bound memo, waits for the withdrawal to settle on Tempo and sends a `zone` Credential. The withdrawal pays its Zone gas in the charge currency, or in the token set with `TempoZone::with_fee_token`. A withdrawal whose payout bounced on Tempo fails without a Credential with `MppError::ZoneWithdrawalBounced`, which MCP clients do not answer by paying another offer since the withdrawal's fees are spent; the challenge may be paid again. If the wait ends first, the payment fails with `MppError::ZoneSettlementPending`, which carries the challenge: paying that challenge again with the same signer resumes the wait instead of withdrawing again (providers with other signers built from clones of one `TempoZone` withdraw for themselves), while a fresh challenge from a re-sent request would withdraw again. `zones` cannot be combined with `relay`, which does not verify Zone credentials. A handler with `relay` also refuses to issue a challenge from `charge_challenge_with_options` whose request carries `methodDetails.zones`, and a relayed charge method rejects `zone` Credentials. A `zone` Credential that carries a `source` is rejected, as mpp-go does, since nothing ties a Zone withdrawal to a source DID. A malformed `methodDetails.zones` reads as no Zones instead of failing the challenge. `TempoMethodDetails` gained a `zones` field and `PayloadType` a `Zone` variant, so exhaustive literals and matches need updating. Zone IDs must be positive and below 421700000, as in mppx, in `zones`, `zone` Credentials and `TempoZone::with_zone_id`, and a Zone's portal address is derived from its literal ID, so a Zone chain ID such as 421700003 is never read as Zone 3.
