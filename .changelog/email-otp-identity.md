Added identity attestations and email OTP as identity providers, as mppx's `mppx/extensions/identity/{attestation,email-otp}` do, behind the new `identity-attestation` and `identity-email-otp` features. `identity::ext::attestation::AttestationVerifier` and `identity::ext::email_otp::EmailOtpVerifier` are `Verifier`s for a route's `RouteIdentity`: they read `Identity-Attestation`, verify the EdDSA JWS against the issuer's JSON Web Key Set (`JwksResolver`, cached for 5 minutes, refetched for an unknown key ID at most every 30 seconds, last good set served for up to an hour while the issuer is down) and check issuer, audience, type, expiry, `max_age` and an `allow` callback, rejecting as `missing`/`invalid` (401, with `Identity-Attestation-Required`), `forbidden` (403) or `unavailable` (503). On the client, `AttestationProver` is a `Prover` (for `IdentityClient` and `ChargeClient::with_identity`) that presents cached attestations only to origins in its scope, never over plain HTTP except to localhost, and resends an unpaid request rejected with `Identity-Attestation-Required` once with a new one; `EmailOtpProver` builds one that obtains an email attestation by having a trusted issuer email a one-time code and asking a callback for it (up to three times).
