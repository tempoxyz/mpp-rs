Added out-of-order session vouchers inside a reorder window. A server opts in with `SessionMethod::with_reorder_window` (`DEFAULT_REORDER_WINDOW` is 30 seconds; windows are capped at `MAX_REORDER_WINDOW_SECS`, one day) and advertises `reorderWindow` in its session Challenges. It then accepts each voucher increment `(after, cumulativeAmount]` once, also below the highest accepted voucher, as long as it is newer than the window, and refuses stale and replayed vouchers as `delta-too-small` without naming the channel's accepted amount. Requests are still charged from the highest voucher. On a store shared by several server instances, a voucher whose write loses a compare-and-swap is applied again to the fresh state, up to eight times. A client paying such a server over a TIP-1034 channel signs each voucher for its own slice of the channel's counter, sends its start as `after`, and releases the payment lock before the request, so concurrent requests from one provider pay over one channel at once; receipts for these payments cost each request its slice. Prepaid vouchers, legacy escrow channels and servers without `reorderWindow` pay serialized as before, and sharing a channel across processes is left to the application. Session voucher payloads gained the optional `after` field, `TempoSessionMethodDetails` gained `reorder_window`, `ChannelState` gained `window`, and `ErrorCode` gained `ConcurrentUpdate` (returned by `ChannelStoreAdapter` for a lost compare-and-swap, previously `Internal`).
