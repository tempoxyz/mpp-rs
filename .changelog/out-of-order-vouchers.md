Added out-of-order session vouchers inside a reorder window. A server opts in with `SessionMethod::with_reorder_window` (`DEFAULT_REORDER_WINDOW` is 30 seconds) and advertises `reorderWindow` in its session challenges. It then accepts each voucher increment `(after, cumulativeAmount]` once, also below the highest accepted voucher, as long as it is newer than the window, and refuses stale and replayed vouchers with `acceptedCumulative` and `requiredCumulative` problem members. Requests are still charged from the highest voucher. A client paying such a server allocates each voucher from the channel's counter (atomically in the channel store, with one `UPDATE ... RETURNING` for `SqliteChannelStore`) and sends it with `after`, so concurrent requests, and processes sharing a store, pay over one channel without waiting for each other. Without `reorderWindow` nothing changes. Session voucher payloads gained the optional `after` field, `TempoSessionMethodDetails` gained `reorder_window`, `ChannelState` gained `window`, `PaymentErrorDetails` gained `accepted_cumulative` and `required_cumulative`, the client `ChannelStore` gained `allocate`, and `PaymentProvider` gained `observe_refusal`.
